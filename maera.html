Maera

A Chimera · Builder & Craftsperson

Maera is a travelling craftsperson whose mixed heritage is as individual as the life she has built. Her strength is useful, but her patience, practical intelligence and knowledge of tools are what make her dependable when work becomes difficult.

What makes her Maera

Her horns do not follow the same curve, and the pale fur on her arms contrasts with the darker tones of her hair and travelling clothes. A worn pack and ready tools distinguish her as someone who expects to be useful wherever she arrives.

Maera is not intended as a template for every Chimera. Their different combinations of features, strengths and needs are part of what makes their people varied.
