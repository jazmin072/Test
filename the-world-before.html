THE STORY

About The World Before

The second novel looks behind the mysteries introduced in Elf Blood. It explores Plu and Selena, the first ages of their worlds, ancient civilizations, and the history whose effects will eventually reach the separated era. What appears inevitable in the present has roots in acts of creation, loyalty and conflict.

The World Before is intended as Book Two; its context deepens the mystery introduced in Elf Blood.
