THE STORY

About The Listening World

Two centuries after the events of the main trilogy, Reyla listens to the world she has chosen to protect. Her journey takes her among communities, forests and distant lands, where understanding can accomplish more than violence.

The Listening World is a companion story within The Chronicles of Plu and Selena. It can be enjoyed on its own, but takes place after Book Three.
