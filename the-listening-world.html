THE STORY

About The Listening World

Reyla’s journey follows the patient work of mediation, the living world and the communities who inhabit it. Her travels include Everlangyrs and the Dark Veil Mountains as she seeks lasting cooperation rather than force. This reflective companion story explores connection, belonging and the time needed for trust to take root.

Part of The Chronicles of Plu and Selena by Jonathan M. Reed.
