About The Listening World

This standalone story explores communities, forests and the quiet language of a living world. It follows its own narrative and does not need to be placed within the timeline of the main trilogy.

The Listening World is a separate work by Jonathan M. Reed, outside the main continuity of The Chronicles of Plu and Selena.
