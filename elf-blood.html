THE STORY

About Elf Blood

Taylor and Breni have shared a secluded life for decades. When Reyla joins their household, the routines that once gave them peace begin to change. Elsewhere, Ray, an agent with an unusual gift for seeing what others cannot, follows evidence that should not exist. Their journeys move toward one another while the world conceals the truth about its own division.

Book One is the entry point. You do not need to read any companion story beforehand.
