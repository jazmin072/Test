THE STORY

About Dark Rain

After the central trilogy, an attack on a Greybridge grain store threatens the fragile peace between rival clans. Missing dwarves, marked attackers and carefully arranged evidence lead Breni and Iven into an investigation involving captives, dangerous equipment and an unexpected power beginning to awaken. This companion story follows Breni’s restraint, loyalty and determination as she searches for the truth.

Part of The Chronicles of Plu and Selena by Jonathan M. Reed.

Reading note: This story takes place after the main trilogy. New readers can begin with Elf Blood to discover the world without later-story spoilers.
