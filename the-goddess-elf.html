THE STORY

About The Goddess Elf

The bonds created across separated realities lead into a new era of difficult choices. Relationships, places and communities must adjust to a changing world, while unfinished conflicts demand care and courage. This final book completes the central trilogy and opens the way for the later companion adventures.

This page avoids revealing Book Three's major turning points. Its story takes place after Books One and Two.
