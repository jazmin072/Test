Privacy Notice

For the Chronicles of Plu and Selena reader website. Updated November 14, 2026.

What you can use without an account

Reading the free Plu’s Creatures handbook, viewing book listings, browsing characters, and exploring artwork do not require registration.

Optional reader accounts

Readers may choose to register using an email sign-in link. Account authentication is provided by Supabase. The site uses your email address to send a requested sign-in link and stores your chosen avatar with your account profile. The site does not ask you to create a password or subscribe to a newsletter.

Authentication services may process technical information necessary to provide secure sessions. Review the provider's privacy information for its handling of data.

Book reviews

If you submit a review, the site collects the selected book, your chosen display name, star rating and review text. Reviews are held for moderation before publication. Only approved reviews display publicly; the form does not request an email address. Avoid entering personal information into your display name or review text.

Website hosting and outside links

Vercel hosts the website and may process technical request information needed to deliver the site. Links to booksellers, image resources and other services may have their own privacy practices. Visiting those links takes you away from this website.

Requests and updates

You may ask the website operator through the author's official contact channels to review or remove a published review, or to request account-data assistance. This notice may be updated when the site’s reader features change.
